Signal Painter

Paint your Wi-Fi onto the floor plan.

Browsers cannot read Wi-Fi signal strength in dBm, on any platform. So this version measures what you actually feel: real download, upload, and ping at every spot you stand.

1
Add your floor plan

Photo of a printed plan or a paper sketch. Or just use the grid.

2
Stand somewhere, tap that spot

Drag to pan, pinch to zoom, tap to place yourself.

3
Run a test, walk, repeat

Each spot paints a cell. Weak spots flag automatically. Save surveys and compare after moving the router.

Runs entirely in this page. Surveys stay on this device. Speed tests use Cloudflare's public endpoints.
